# Original flagship: approved design → fabric substitution → verified handoff

## Studio workflow

In **Materials**, record an offer or import a supplier CSV of up to 50 rows and 60 KB. The importer validates the whole file before recording it. Required columns are `name`, `evidence`, `price_per_metre`, `currency` and `observed_on` (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Optional columns are `available` (`true`, `false`, `unknown`), `stretch_percent`, `width_cm`, `gsm`, `composition` and `supersedes_offer_id`. Leave unmeasured properties blank. Each imported offer retains the supplied evidence and observation date. Live supplier inventory and automated external catalog discovery remain open.

Compare offers against minimum stretch and any required width or maximum weight. A missing required measurement gives an unknown result, while an explicit failure gives an incompatible result. Estimate costs in one currency using decimal arithmetic. The stored comparison contains the exact inputs, candidates, costs and exclusions.

Choose a compatible offer to stage a substitution. The review shows the estimate and affected artifacts, including downstream dependencies. Replacing a supplier offer invalidates selection of its previous record, even after a proposal has been staged. Committing creates an unapproved revision. After aesthetic approval, prepare a new JSON handoff draft to include the selected offer and the comparison estimate bound to that revision. The draft calls for patternmaker review.

Baseline: approved DGT revision R with locked neckline and embroidery placement. Supplier offer becomes unavailable or price increases. A catalog lookup returns candidate material records with composition, width, GSM/stretch/drape *only where measured*, price+currency+unit, retrieved timestamp and inventory evidence. Candidate choice compares hard feasibility requirements and aesthetic preferences separately; any unknown hard requirement blocks auto-recommendation. Cost engine calculates length × unit price + defined wastage + trim + other line items, with rounding and exclusions explicit. New candidate proposed as child revision; invalidates visual preview, BOM, technical notes and spec draft where dependency edges point to changed fabric. Regenerate drafts only. Preserve approver identity, approval purpose and exact new revision hash. Flag manufacturer review for all speculative garment construction impacts. Acceptance: no locked alterations; no made-up fabric physics or stock; correct cost and stale detection; no silent side effects.
